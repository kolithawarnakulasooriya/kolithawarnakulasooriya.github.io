# C04 publication citation review

Checked **2026-11-01**. Scope is the fourteen publication records already present in the website. No absent 2027, under-review or other new papers were added, and no PDF was edited.

## Meaning of verification

- **Verified:** primary publisher-deposited metadata or an official workshop paper establishes the core title, ordered authors, venue, citation year and destination identity.
- **Partial:** primary author/university material supports the citation, but original proceedings or a published byline remain unavailable. These records are retained with their limits and excluded from verified-publication counts.
- This is citation verification, not a claim that every publisher landing page opens without a subscription, bot check or network restriction. It does not establish acceptance status for unrelated papers, author contribution percentages or a total number of first-author publications.

The standalone data source is `src/app/publications/data.ts`: fourteen stable IDs, ordered author arrays, year/venue/kind and dated evidence. Twelve core citation records are verified; two remain partial. No aggregate publication count is added to the website by this change.

## Source authority and year conventions

For eleven DOI records, the full publisher-deposited Crossref response was read directly, including title, all ordered author names, container title, publication dates and work type. Exact article/document IDs match the retained destinations. Conference years follow their publication/proceedings records. Published spelling and initials are retained rather than replaced with the website owner's preferred name.

The comparative-algorithm article is the one special year case: Springer says first published online **5 December 2024**, while its recommended volume citation is **Evolutionary Intelligence 18, article 18 (2025)**. The dataset uses **2025** for that citation and explicitly records the earlier online date. This is not a contradiction or an invented extra publication.
